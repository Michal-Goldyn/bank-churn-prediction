# Summary for the Bank Director

## Top 5 Findings

1. The Complain column is almost identical to the Exited column (matching in 9,986 
   out of 10,000 cases) - this is data leakage and cannot be used in the predictive model.

2. Customers in Germany aged 45-60 churn at a 67% rate - the worst segment in the 
   entire bank, more than three times the average churn rate.

3. Customers with 3 or 4 bank products churn at 83-100%, while customers with 1-2 
   products churn at only 8-28% - something is seriously wrong with these products.

4. Customers who leave are on average 7 years older (45) than those who stay (37) - the strongest factor among all tested.

5. Customers who leave have an average balance 18,000 higher than those who stay 
(91k vs 73k) - wealthier customers are more likely to switch banks.

## Recommendations

1. Investigate and address the issues affecting senior customers in Germany - 
this is the single most critical segment to fix.

2. Review the terms and conditions of products 3 and 4 - extreme churn rates 
suggest these products are actively driving customers away.

3. Review general banking conditions for older customers - age is the strongest churn predictor, and current offerings may not meet their needs.
